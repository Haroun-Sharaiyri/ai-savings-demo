Proof of AI Savings

a verification prototype
1

Generate Data

Create 1,200 fake contact-center conversations — 600 baseline ("before AI") and 600 "after AI" — using a seeded random generator so the data is the same every time you reload.

2

Scramble PII

Replace employee IDs and customer phone numbers with HMAC-SHA256 codes so the data can be audited without exposing anyone's identity.

3

Measure & Quality Gate

Compare before vs. after across every channel. Calculate hours saved per day, then check quality: a channel only passes if satisfaction drops by 2 points or less and first-contact resolution does not drop.

4

Lock (Merkle Tree)

Hash every clean record into a leaf. Pair leaves into a binary tree until a single root hash remains. That root is a fingerprint of every record: change one byte anywhere and the root changes too.

5

Prove & Tamper

Pick any record and verify it's included in the tree. Then tamper with one field and see the proof break instantly.

6

Anchor

Download a receipt containing the Merkle root, record count, and verified savings. You could timestamp this at opentimestamps.org to prove when the data was locked.
